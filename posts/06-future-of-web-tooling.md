## The Native Web Tooling Renaissance

A profound shift is taking place across frontend infrastructure. The days of JavaScript-based bundling chains are giving way to high-performance native binaries written in **Rust** and **Go**.

---

### The New Vanguard

* **Trunk**: The premier WASM bundler for Rust web apps.
* **SWC & Biome**: Blazing-fast JavaScript/TypeScript compilers written in Rust.
* **Turbopack**: Vercel's Rust-based successor to Webpack.
* **Rspack**: High-performance bundler with Webpack interoperability.

```
Compilation Times (Cold Start):
[=====================] Webpack (Node.js) ~ 12.4s
[==]                    Trunk / Rust      ~ 0.38s
```

### Conclusion

The tools of web development are finally matching the performance expectations of systems programming. By compiling directly to WebAssembly and native machine code, we unlock instant feedback loops.
